# Honest limitations

Shown in the UI and the README. Honest labelling is worth 10% of the score —
and it's the cheapest 10 points available.

## What's real

- Escrow locks and settlements on Masumi **preprod** (Cardano testnet):
  real state transitions, real tx hashes, real explorer links.
- Publisher agents are identified via the registry (DIDs, service cards).
- The auction mechanism is real: tender → sealed bids → allocation from bids
  → settlement against measured outcomes.

## What's simulated (labelled SIMULATED in the UI)

- The shop and its conversion events: a simulated shop emits signed
  conversion events. Attribution is first-touch and demo-simple.
- Traffic serving: impressions are generated by the scenario script.
- Bot flood: DevNewsletter's zero-outcome traffic is scripted — the
  *mechanism* (gate check → refund) is what's on show.

## What's pre-recorded (labelled PRE-RECORDED)

- `DEMO_MODE=canned` replays a recorded successful run. Used only as the
  lifeline if live systems fail.

## Known simplifications (stated, not hidden)

- Publishers are our policy-bound agents; third-party settlement would use
  the Disputed/admin path (stated in UI, not hidden).
- Quotes are scripted; the sealed-bid *mechanism* is what's demonstrated.
- The binary performance gate (≥5 verified outcomes / 1k impressions) is a
  policy choice, not a law of nature — crude next to real media-mix modeling.
- Attribution is first-touch; production needs multi-touch.
- Signed outcomes assume the shop key is safe — a compromised shop mints
  conversions.
- On-chain data is hashes and references only; raw reports stay off-chain.
